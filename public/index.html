Five kinds of work, one way of working

02( Services )Survey, engineering and GIS for public agencies and the developers who build next to them.

  • 01

    Civil engineering

    Municipal design, paving, drainage and utility design, site development, corridor studies and multi-modal design.

  • 02

    Land surveying

    Boundary and title surveys, plats, topographic design surveys, construction staking and right-of-way mapping.

  • 03

    Hydrology and hydraulics

    HEC-HMS and HEC-RAS modeling, floodplain and drainage studies, culvert and bridge hydraulics.

  • 04

    GIS and geospatial

    Planning, mapping and spatial analysis that put survey, utility and parcel data on one base.

  • 05

    Construction administration

    Submittal review, on-site inspection, RFI responses and value engineering by the engineers who drew the plans.
