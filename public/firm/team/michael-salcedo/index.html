Background

02( Experience )President of Salcedo Group, Inc.

Michael Salcedo is the President of SGI and has over twenty years of experience in engineering, surveying and GIS. His design experience on public and private projects includes site development, water distribution, wastewater collection, storm drainage, roadways, trail development, hydrology and hydraulics, GIS planning and mapping, platting, topographic and boundary surveying, preparation of easements and construction administration.

“As surveyor, I’m the first person who puts boots on ground. We survey the project, measure the lot, then we bring it into the office and do all the engineering design work.”
