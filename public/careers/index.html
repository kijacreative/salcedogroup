What the work looks like

02( At SGI )Survey and design in the same building.

SGI’s two survey crews and its design staff work in the same office, so the people who measure a site and the people who design it work side by side.

Recent projects include Pacific Plaza and Harold Simmons Park in Dallas, the Camp Wisdom Road Reconstruction for Dallas County and the Grand Prairie Municipal Campus.
