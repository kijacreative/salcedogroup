02( Questions )The five things city engineers and developers ask us first.

( Area )

Where do you work?

Dallas, Dallas County, Grand Prairie, Fort Worth and Balch Springs, with professional engineering licensure in Texas, Oklahoma and Colorado.

( Survey )

Do you self-perform survey?

Yes. Two in-house survey crews handle boundary, topographic and construction staking, so survey and design never leave the building.

( Seals )

When does a project need an RPLS and when a PE?

Boundary surveys and plats are sealed by a Registered Professional Land Surveyor; engineering plans are sealed by a Professional Engineer. Both SGI principals hold both registrations.

( Goals )

Does SGI count toward M/DBE participation goals?

SGI is NCTRCA certified M/DBE, a federal Small Disadvantaged Business and TxDOT precertified. Certificates are available on request.
