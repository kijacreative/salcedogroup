Boots on the ground before a line is drawn

02( Survey )Every SGI project starts with our own crews in the field. What follows is a sealed survey, a design and a set of plans from the same hands.

  1. 01( Boundary )

    Record research, field notes and boundary analysis, sealed by a Registered Professional Land Surveyor.

  2. 02( Topographic )

    Topographic design surveys and 3D CAD modeling, so design starts from measured ground rather than record drawings.

  3. 03( Design )

    The same firm carries the survey into paving, drainage and utility design. No handoff and no subconsultant markup.

  4. 04( Staking )

    Our crews stake what we drew and inspect it through construction, then close out with as-builts.

Built in the order a project is built

03( Order of work )Every job runs the same five stages, and the same two principals stay on it from the first stake to the last as-built.

  1. 01

    Survey

    Boundary, topographic and utility survey by our own crews. Record research, field notes and a sealed plat.

    RPLS

  2. 02

    Preliminary design

    Alignments, drainage and hydraulic models, and the agency and neighborhood meetings that settle them.

    PE · CFM

  3. 03

    Final design

    Plans, specifications and estimates, sealed by the engineer of record and kept inside the existing right of way wherever we can.

    PE of record

  4. 04

    Review and bid

    City, county, Corps and TxDOT review, then bid-phase support until a contractor is under contract.

    Principal

  5. 05

    Construction

    Staking by the crew that surveyed it. Submittal review, on-site inspection, RFIs and value engineering through closeout.

    RPLS · field
