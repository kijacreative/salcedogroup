01.04( Civil engineering )

Utility design.

Water and wastewater design and relocation, coordinated with every utility owner on the corridor.

Request a proposal(214) 412-3122

What it involves

  • 01Water distribution design
  • 02Wastewater collection design
  • 03Utility relocation
  • 04Utility conflict assessment

02( How SGI does it )

On Beckley at Commerce, SGI designed the relocation of 5,600 linear feet of water and wastewater pipeline ranging from 8 to 24 inches in diameter.

On the Hi Line Connection, utility relocation sat alongside paving and drainage, with coordination across City and State stakeholders including TxDOT, the North Texas Tollway Authority and ONCOR Electric. On Harold Simmons Park, SGI is assessing the major utility conflicts that could affect the park’s connections.
