Questions

04( Questions )What owners and agencies ask first.

Does SGI work for private developers?

Yes. Recent private work includes Peachtree Apartments in Balch Springs, Treehaus Apartments, Bishop Canopy, Aventine at Kessler Park and Munger Court in Dallas, and Hotel Revel in Fort Worth.

Can SGI survey, plat and design the same site?

Yes. Survey crews, platting and civil design are all in house, so one firm carries a site from the boundary survey to construction documents.

Where does SGI do land development work?

Across Dallas-Fort Worth, including Dallas, Fort Worth, Sunnyvale and Grand Prairie.
