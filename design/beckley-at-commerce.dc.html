What SGI did

02( Project )

SGI provided civil engineering design and land surveying for the reconstruction of the intersection of North Beckley Avenue and Commerce Street, an important West Dallas gateway. This project consisted of reconstructing a 2-lane undivided, grade separated half-clover leaf intersection to a 4-lane divided at-grade signalized intersection, with separated bike lanes, and wide pedestrian areas. SGI provided drainage design using LID techniques including providing urban rain gardens in lieu of traditional curb inlets. A major storm drainage system was constructed to provide drainage relief for upstream portions of West Dallas. SGI also provided design for relocation of 5,600 LF of water and wastewater pipeline ranging in size from 8”-24” diameter.

The Beckley/Commerce intersection reconstruction was a community driven project and one of Dallas’ first complete and green streets incorporating Low-Impact Development.

SGI met with neighborhood stakeholders in person and presented a preliminary design in a community meeting. One major concern from the community involved preservation of an old Pecan Tree located on the south side of the intersection. SGI met with community leaders, and enlisted certified arborists to provide direction on methods of preservation of this important tree. The roadway alignment was modified specifically to save this tree. After forging community consensus, our team helped bring the design from concept to final construction documents. The project introduces rain gardens, pedestrian refuges in medians, bus stops, buffered bike lanes, wide and shaded sidewalks, and Trinity River wayfinding signage.
