Working in Grand Prairie

03( Local process )

SGI’s office is in Grand Prairie, 15 minutes southwest of downtown Dallas. The office held its ribbon cutting in 2019.

On the Municipal Campus, SGI performed civil site design, topographic surveying, platting and construction administration. The platting covered record research, field notes and boundary analysis for a historic portion of downtown.
