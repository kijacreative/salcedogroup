04( Questions )What owners and agencies ask first.

Why map the right of way before design?

The right of way sets what can be built without acquisition. Mapping it first lets the design fit the corridor rather than the other way around.

Has SGI avoided right-of-way acquisition on a project?

Yes. On Camp Wisdom Road, SGI’s design keeps all improvements inside the existing right of way, so no acquisition is needed.
