Working in City of Dallas

03( Local process )

SGI has worked on some of the largest and most visible projects in Dallas, including the Mill Creek diversion tunnel in the Uptown and McKinney Avenue area and Pacific Plaza downtown. Luis M. Salcedo’s earlier City of Dallas work includes the Love Field Drainage Master Plan, the Bachman Lake Study, Main Street downtown, the Dallas Streetcar and presedimentation basins at the Elm Fork Water Treatment Plant.
