What SGI did

02( Project )

SGI is providing Civil Engineering, Land Surveying, and Planning services as a sub-consultant to Brooklyn, NY based Michael Van Valkenburgh Associates (MVVA), for the design and implementation of Harold Simmons Park in the Dallas Floodway. This project spans a long history within the City of Dallas, as a derivative of the Balanced Vision Plan; a complex and multi-faceted design project at the doorstep of Downtown Dallas. Harold Simmons Park is the result of a $50 Million donation provided by the Simmons Family, for the purpose of implementing this long sought-after park in Dallas.

SGI’s responsibility consists of civil engineering, infrastructure design, drainage analysis, and multi-agency coordination for this iconic park. SGI is also providing civil engineering design of two gateway overlooks into the floodway, located on either east/west levees. SGI’s civil engineering effort includes bank stabilization techniques, channel or embankment armoring, paving and drainage design, utility design or relocations, earth mass grading and volume calculations. This project will incorporate a variety of Low-Impact Development techniques including bio-swales, rain gardens, wetlands mediation, and is designed for inundation to operate as a traditional floodway system.

Additionally, SGI is working with the Trinity Park Conservancy on an Urban Design initiative to study impacts due to access, equity, or opportunities of the park to the connecting communities. SGI is informing the infrastructure impacts of the connections to the community, such as pedestrian, bicycle, or vehicular access. SGI is also assessing the major utility conflicts which could potentially affect the project connections.
