02( How SGI does it )

“As surveyor, I’m the first person who puts boots on ground,” says Michael Salcedo. “We survey the project, measure the lot, then we bring it into the office and do all the engineering design work.”

SGI’s surveys feed directly into 3D CAD models that the engineers use for grading, drainage and earthwork. On Harold Simmons Park, that includes earth mass grading and volume calculations in the Trinity River floodway.

Questions

04( Questions )What owners and agencies ask first.

Why use the same firm for survey and design?

There is no handoff. The engineers design from a survey their own crews collected, and questions about the ground are answered down the hall.

What is a 3D CAD surface model used for?

Grading, drainage design and earthwork quantities. The model lets the engineers test grades and calculate cut and fill before construction.
