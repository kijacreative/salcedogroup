What SGI did

02( Project )

The Circuit Trail Conservancy (CTC) Hi Line Connection is an approximately one mile trail project that connects two of Dallas’ most precious public resources - The Katy Trail and Trinity Strand Trail. Within the project’s scope are raised bike lanes, expanded pedestrian streetscape conditions and the relocation of roadways and existing infrastructure.

Among the improvements to Hi Line Drive’s streetscape, the project will also feature public art installations, new state of the art pedestrian crossing signalization, and an iconic pedestrian bridge over a major vehicular intersection that will be designed and engineered by SWA.

SGI is performing all civil engineering and land surveying scope for this project, including paving, drainage, utility relocation, striping, and grading. Given the complexity of the project, SGI is assisting coordinating with City and State level stakeholders including Parks Department, Transportation, TxDOT, North Texas Tollway Authority, ONCOR Electric, and many individual private real-estate stakeholders.
