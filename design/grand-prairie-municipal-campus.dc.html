What SGI did

02( Project )

SGI is the civil engineer of record and land surveyor for the design and construction of the City of Grand Prairie Municipal Campus, anchored by a new City Hall. This multi-phase project consists of 11 acres of publicly owned land in the center of Downtown Grand Prairie. SGI performed civil site design including, paving, grading, drainage, utility design & relocation, erosion protection, topographic surveying, platting, and construction administration.

A major design element features an urban rain garden piercing the front face of the new City Hall, collecting upstream runoff from a local drainage system, this rain garden signifies the headwater of Henry Branch, a tributary to Cottonwood Branch in Grand Prairie. The linear rain garden extends as a central focal point for pedestrian access, and serves to provide hydraulic capacity for the 11 acre catchment.

SGI performed platting services, consisting of record research, writing field notes, and performing boundary analysis for this historic portion of downtown. SGI provided construction administration throughout the project, including reviewing contractor submittals, on-site inspection, responding to RFI’s, and Value Engineering design. This project is complete. Phase 2 is currently in the design phase.
