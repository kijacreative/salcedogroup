Construction administration in detail

03( In detail )Four parts of construction administration, each handled by the engineers who drew the plans.

Draft for SGI reviewCopy drafted from the SGI Statement of Qualifications. Confirm facts and wording before publishing.

01

Submittal review

Shop drawings and contractor submittals checked against the design intent by the engineers of record.

  • Shop drawing review
  • Material and product submittals
  • Resubmittal tracking
  • Coordination with the owner

SGI provided construction administration throughout the Grand Prairie Municipal Campus, including reviewing contractor submittals, on-site inspection, responding to RFIs and value engineering design.

02

On-site inspection

Field inspection by the engineers of record, from mobilization through closeout.

  • Site visits and observation
  • Field engineering
  • Field reports
  • Punch lists

Field engineering and inspection are part of SGI’s core capabilities. On the Grand Prairie Municipal Campus, on-site inspection was part of SGI’s construction administration throughout the project.

Michael Salcedo describes the work as running “from the first one on the site to the last one to leave.”

03

RFI responses

Answers to contractor requests for information from the people who drew the plans.

  • RFI tracking and responses
  • Clarification sketches
  • Field condition changes
  • Coordination with the owner and architect

Responding to RFIs was part of SGI’s construction administration on the Grand Prairie Municipal Campus. Because the survey and the design came from SGI, questions about existing conditions and intent go to the same team.

04

Value engineering

Alternatives that hold the design intent and the budget.

  • Cost-saving alternatives
  • Constructability review
  • Material and method options
  • Redesign where it pays

SGI aims for designs that are fiscally responsible and innovative. On the Grand Prairie Municipal Campus, value engineering design was part of construction administration.

Some of the largest savings come during design. On Camp Wisdom Road, keeping every improvement inside the existing right of way meant no right-of-way acquisition was needed.

Questions

Who reviews submittals on SGI projects?

The engineers who designed the project.

Has SGI provided full construction administration?

Yes. On the Grand Prairie Municipal Campus, SGI reviewed submittals, inspected on site, answered RFIs and provided value engineering.

Does the design engineer inspect construction?

On SGI projects with construction administration, yes. The engineers of record visit the site and respond to field conditions.

When does inspection start?

It is set by the contract; SGI’s construction administration typically runs from mobilization through closeout.

Who answers RFIs on SGI-designed projects?

The SGI engineers who prepared the plans.

Why does that matter?

The answer comes from the people who know why each detail was drawn, which keeps responses quick and consistent with the design.

When does value engineering happen?

During design and again during construction, when a contractor proposes alternatives or field conditions change.

Has SGI value-engineered a public project?

Yes. Value engineering design was part of SGI’s construction administration on the Grand Prairie Municipal Campus.
