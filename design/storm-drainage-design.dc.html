Questions

04( Questions )What owners and agencies ask first.

What is low-impact development drainage?

Drainage that slows and treats runoff with planted features such as rain gardens and bioswales, rather than sending all of it straight into inlets and pipe. SGI used rain gardens in place of curb inlets at Beckley at Commerce.

Does SGI do the drainage design and the hydraulic model?

Yes. Storm drainage design and hydrology and hydraulics are both done in house, so the pipe network is sized against the same analysis.

How is a detention basin sized?

By comparing peak runoff before and after development in a hydrologic model, then sizing storage and the outlet so the release meets the city’s criteria.

Can landscape features provide storage?

Yes. Rain gardens and bioswales can hold runoff. The Grand Prairie Municipal Campus rain garden provides hydraulic capacity for its catchment.
