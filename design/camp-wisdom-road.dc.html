SGI is the prime consultant for this $21,000,000 Dallas County project. The existing Camp Wisdom roadway is a 2-lane asphalt road located in both Grand Prairie and Dallas. The project will improve the 1.8 mile stretch of road to a 4-lane concrete pavement thoroughfare with upgraded drainage, new bridge at Mountain Creek and a multi-use hike/biking trail.

The roadway is immediately downstream of Joe Pool Lake and therefore requires close coordination with the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers, as well as both cities. The hydrology and hydraulic analysis included developing HEC-HMS and HEC-RAS models to incorporate the Joe Pool Lake spillway and outfall structures. The existing 2-lane bridge over Mountain Creek will be replaced with a wider, 4-lane bridge designed to pass the 100-year flood.

A major design constraint is to keep all proposed improvements within the existing ROW, to avoid requesting additional ROW from the Corps. SGI’s design accomplishes this major objective and no ROW acquisition is needed, incorporating MSE walls to prevent construction activities from encroaching Corps Property. The design is complete, and the project was bid in the Summer of 2019. Construction is currently underway.
