02( How SGI does it )

For Camp Wisdom Road, immediately downstream of Joe Pool Lake, SGI developed HEC-HMS and HEC-RAS models that incorporate the Joe Pool Lake spillway and outfall structures. The new 4-lane bridge over Mountain Creek is designed to pass the 100-year flood.

Luis M. Salcedo, a Certified Floodplain Manager, has worked on some of Dallas’ largest drainage studies, including the Love Field Drainage Master Plan, the Bachman Lake Study and the Mill Creek Tunnel.
