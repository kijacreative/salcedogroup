Background

02( Experience )Chairman and CEO of Salcedo Group, Inc.

As Chairman and CEO of Salcedo Group, Inc., Luis Salcedo brings forty-five years of experience in civil engineering and surveying. His work includes civil, utility, infrastructure and drainage design, street improvements, hydrology and hydraulics analysis and design, GIS, design surveys, boundary surveys and plats.

He has worked on some of the largest public projects in the City of Dallas, including the Love Field Drainage Master Plan, the Bachman Lake Study, the Mill Creek Tunnel, the Trinity River Corridor Dream Team Charrette, the Elm Fork Water Treatment Plant presedimentation basins, Main Street in downtown Dallas, the Beckley and Commerce intersection and the Dallas Streetcar.
